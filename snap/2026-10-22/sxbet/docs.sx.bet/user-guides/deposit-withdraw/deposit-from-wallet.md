# Deposit From Crypto Wallet

> Learn how to deposit USDC on SX Bet using your crypto wallet.

It's easy to go from any token on any chain to USDC on SX Bet. If you have crypto in your wallet already, you can use it to deposit USDC in seconds.

1. Click "Deposit"
2. Select the "Wallet" option. From the list, select the type of wallet where your crypto balance is.

![](https://mintcdn.com/sxbet-9c561d83/nTchO44e8z9Jwtov/public/user-guides/deposit_from_wallet.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=nTchO44e8z9Jwtov\&q=85\&s=3fb264e69d4ea720cb3238c44681d19b)

3. Enter the amount of USDC you want to deposit and click continue.

> [!NOTE]
>
> If you have multiple tokens in your wallet, you may click "more options" to select which token balance to use.

![](https://mintcdn.com/sxbet-9c561d83/nTchO44e8z9Jwtov/public/user-guides/select_token_wallet_deposit.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=nTchO44e8z9Jwtov\&q=85\&s=d8af7e65bb4e741382e2b77dcb3e497e)

4. Click "Deposit Now" and confirm the transaction in your wallet.

![](https://mintcdn.com/sxbet-9c561d83/nTchO44e8z9Jwtov/public/user-guides/deposit_now_metamask.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=nTchO44e8z9Jwtov\&q=85\&s=77ed8592c3aded2f4e6269a13262d0c3)

Your transaction should be complete within a few seconds! You're all set to start betting.

![](https://mintcdn.com/sxbet-9c561d83/nTchO44e8z9Jwtov/public/user-guides/deposit_wallet_success.png?fit=max\&auto=format\&n=nTchO44e8z9Jwtov\&q=85\&s=9513bfffd44d613f345e13c591b87a02)
