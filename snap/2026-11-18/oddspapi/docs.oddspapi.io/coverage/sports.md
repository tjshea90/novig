## Reading the table

* **Fixtures** are match- or event-level markets (a single game, bout, or match), streamed on the [`fixtures`](https://docs.oddspapi.io/websocket/channels/fixtures) and [`odds`](https://docs.oddspapi.io/websocket/channels/odds) channels.
* **Futures** are outright / season-long or topic-level markets (league winner, tournament, election, etc.), streamed on the [`futures`](https://docs.oddspapi.io/websocket/channels/futures) and `oddsFutures` channels.
* `sportId` **69–78** are **prediction-market topics** (politics, elections, finance, crypto, weather, …) — these exist only as futures/outright markets and feed the prediction-market and exchange use cases.
* `sportId` **79–81** are **racing codes** (horse, greyhound, harness) — covered as **ante-post futures** (one future per ante-post market), not as per-race fixtures.
* A **market type count** of `—` means no fixture-market taxonomy is published for that sport yet. The sport can still be covered; see [Markets](https://docs.oddspapi.io/coverage/markets.md).

> [!NOTE]
>
> A sport being covered is not a promise that every market type or every bookmaker is available for it. Market breadth is per sport ([Markets](https://docs.oddspapi.io/coverage/markets.md)), and which books quote a given fixture comes from the odds themselves — read it from `GET /fixtures/odds`, not from a coverage table.

## Identifiers

`sportId` is stable and language-independent; the display name follows the request's [language prefix](https://docs.oddspapi.io/api-reference/concepts.md#language-prefix). It is also the **first two digits** of downstream `marketId`, `outcomeId`, and `fixtureId` values — see [Core Concepts](https://docs.oddspapi.io/api-reference/concepts.md#id-structure).

Discover the list available to your key with [`GET /sports`](https://docs.oddspapi.io/api-reference/common/get-sports).
