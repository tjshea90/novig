## Checked and unchanged

Every `/v3/...` path the app calls (`/v3/ws`, `/v3/limits`, `/v3/echo`, `/v3/orders`, `/v3/orders/batch`, `/v3/orders/{id}`, `/v3/portfolio/fills`, `/v3/portfolio/positions`, `/v3/keys/{id}`, `/v3/catalog/events`, `/v3/catalog/markets`, `/v3/catalog/markets/{id}/book`, `/v3/public/catalog/events|markets`, `/v3/account/subaccounts*`) is in the current spec. Every field the spec lists for events, markets, outcomes, orders, fills, positions, limits, books and trades is read by the app except the new ones above. Websocket verbs (`subscribe unsubscribe snapshot status query_markets query_events place cancel cancel_all`), channel costs (lifecycle 1, trades 4, bbo 8, book 16, orders 1, positions 1; upgrade 32), nonce rules, 15 s heartbeat, FMV settlement ("a bare decimal string": handled by `ApiSettler`), event and order lifecycles (`DELAYED` reopens with a cleared book; every `GOLIVE` voids resting orders), the other throttle buckets (place 256/8, cancel 256/16, read 64/16, account 64/8, history 512/4), error codes (451 location, 423 locked, 403 edge HTML), the signing scheme and vectors, deeplink formats, fee formula `c·P·(1−P)`, "fees are charged exactly, no cent floor", Maker Credit 50% of the taker fee earned only on in-game fills. New pages with nothing to build on: Exchange Data CSVs at data.novig.com (daily trades/markets files, published ~5 a.m. ET), RFQ parlay pricing for liquidity providers, affiliates (odds screens, parlay pricing, futures).

## Not changed in the app, on purpose

`book` stays the subscription channel (see the `bbo` row). Futures fee schedule (0.06, ALWAYS) is irrelevant: futures are out of the app.
