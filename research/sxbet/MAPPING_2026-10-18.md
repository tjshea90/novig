Sources: the whole docs site saved in `docs_2026-10-18/` (102 pages + the OpenAPI spec `openapi_2026-10-18.json`), help.sx.bet's sport rules (`BETTING_RULES_2026-10-18.md`), and REAL public answers pulled today into `mapping_2026-10-18/` (sports, 1,776 leagues, 3,260 teams, 11,676 active markets of 579 events, Novig's 367 open events, the engine's `sxlive` journal). ## 1. The id hierarchy
sport (`sportId`, 29: 1 Basketball, 2 Hockey, 3 Baseball, 5 Soccer, 6 Tennis, 7 MMA, 8 Football, 9 E Sports, 13 Boxing, 28 InplayLIVE (novelty), ...) -> league (`leagueId`, 1,776, 66 with events now; one league per tennis tournament) -> **event** (`sportXeventId`, e.g. `L18876509`; the same id on `fixtures:*`, `/live-scores`, `/fixture/*`, `main_line:global` and every market of the game) -> **market** (`marketHash`, 0x..64 hex; the id the odds channel uses) -> two outcomes (`outcomeOneName`/`outcomeTwoName`, a void outcome). Teams: `/teams` (3,260; `id`, `name`, `sport`), referenced by `participantOneId`/`participantTwoId` on a market. A market's own `teamOneName`/`teamTwoName` are the names to use (243 of the markets carry a name that differs from the `/teams` table's, so the table is NOT the source of truth).

| SX field | meaning | Vigilant |
| :- | :- | :- |
| `sportXeventId` | the game | `RefEvent.id = "sx:<id>"` |
| `teamOneName` / `teamTwoName` | **US sports: teamOne = AWAY, teamTwo = HOME** (Dallas Cowboys | Philadelphia Eagles = Dallas at Philadelphia, verified on NFL); soccer: home first is NOT assumed, the planner tries both orders; tennis/MMA: the two players | `RefEvent.away` / `.home` (`SxBetConvert.events`) |
| `gameTime` | scheduled start, UNIX **seconds** | `startMs = gameTime * 1000` |
| `type`, `line` | market type (section 4), the first outcome's handicap or the total | `LineKind`, `RefQuote.points` |
| `outcomeOneName` | "Over 49.5" / "Dallas Cowboys +2.5" / the team | side of the quote |
| `liveEnabled`, `status` | in-play betting allowed; `ACTIVE`/`INACTIVE` | live lane |
| `mainLine` | the main line of that type | prefer for fair |
| `marketMeta` | JSON string; player props carry `player{name,id,participantId}` | prop mapping |
| odds | implied probability x 10^20 in the MAKER frame; taker price = 1 - maker price | `SxBetConvert.takerBook` |

## 3. Team names: what the real data says (167 of 579 SX events matched to Novig's 367 open events; the rest start weeks out, beyond Novig's list)
- NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, WNBA: names equal Novig's ("L.A. Lakers" vs "Los Angeles Lakers" is the only recurring spelling, handled by `TeamMatcher` PHRASES).
- **NCAAF: SX adds the MASCOT** ("Marshall Thundering Herd", "Sam Houston Bearkats", "Florida International Panthers") where Novig has the school ("Marshall", "Sam Houston State", "Florida International"). Fuzzy-matched 3 of 4 near games; `TeamMatcher` handles mascots (its own State/mascot rule) and `SxBetMappingTest` pins real pairs.
- Soccer: accents and full club names differ ("FC Bayern Munich" / "FC Bayern München", "Inter Milan" / "FC Internazionale Milano", "Napoli" / "SSC Napoli"); 91 fuzzy pairs are in `mapping_2026-10-18/name_aliases_fuzzy.json`.
- Tennis: SX "Firstname Lastname" vs Novig "Firstname Lastname" with accents; tournament leagues differ, so tennis is matched by players and the start time (all 69 tennis pairs matched, 54 of them fuzzy).
- Away/home order differs by sport: Novig's description is always "Away @ Home" for US sports.

## 5. Live (the part TCL111f needs): REAL findings
- **In-play markets exist and stay ACTIVE**: 567 markets of games already started (NFL Jacksonville v Houston 132 minutes in, EPL Nottingham Forest v Arsenal 12 minutes in, Ligue 1, La Liga...) are `status ACTIVE`, `liveEnabled true`, so SX quotes in play (the docs' "sweeps resting orders when a fixture goes In progress" means makers repost; `developers_betting-delays.md` has the delay). `fixtures:global` status: 1 not started, 2 in progress, 3 finished, 6 interrupted, 9 about to start.
- `fixtures:live_scores` carries NO team names: `{teamOneScore, teamTwoScore, sportXeventId, currentPeriod, periodTime (seconds into the period; "-1" = n/a or finished), sportId, leagueId, periods[{label,isFinished,teamOneScore,teamTwoScore}], extra: JSON string [{Name:"Turn"...}]}`: join by `sportXeventId` to the market list (teams, start, league). Its volume: ~33 frames a minute across all sports (5,843 in 177 minutes on a Sunday morning), 17,008 in 127 minutes at game time.
- `best_odds_v3:global` carries `marketHash` + the two maker levels only: join by `marketHash` to the market (event, type, line, outcome names). 3,000 sampled in 2 hours (the recorder's cap).
- `main_line:global`: `[{sportXeventId, marketType, marketHash, updateTime(ms)}]` says which hash is the main line of a type.
- Live scores are sport-specific: football quarters ("4th Quarter", "Full time"), soccer halves with seconds (`periodTime "4455"`), `isFinished` per period.

## 6. What to build from this (TCL111f, next): `SxBetLiveFeed : PinnFeedSource`
Events and markets from `/markets/active?liveOnly=true`-style reads (pageSize max 100) per league, kept in a hash->market and event->teams index; odds from `SxBetStream` books (already stored, `watch()` the live hashes); the score and clock from `stream.scores`; fair = the de-vigged pair of the live top of book (`SxBetConvert.pair`, min top $50, max overround 6%); frames in the Pinnodds shape like `BoltLiveFeed`, `PinnFeedChoice.SX`; paper first; REAL orders behind a new gate like `boltLiveReal`. Team-name join to Novig goes through the existing `TeamMatcher` (mascots, accents), tested with the real pairs in `SxBetMappingTest`.
