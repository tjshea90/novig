What changed

  1. Novig scan screening from the catalog. One signed catalog request lists up to 5,000 markets with each side's best bid. The engine uses those to screen every market at once, then reads the full order book only for the +EV ones before anything is shown or bet. The cold scan's Novig step was 13,727 one-by-one reads over 420 s. The public (cached) route is never used for this.
  2. Novig fees. The fallback fee table now has the Oct 22 Maker Credit cut (25% live, 50% futures). Live markets already read their own fee.
  3. PropLine bulk props. A whole slate's props in one call per sport, not one per game, with a per-game fallback if the bulk answer fails. Steals + blocks props map to Novig's relisted STEALS_BLOCKS.
  4. Injuries for free. PropLine's injury report (free on any key) is asked first; ParlayAPI's 1-credit report only covers what PropLine didn't.
  5. No double-counted books. BoltOdds' "consensus" line and DraftKings Predictions / FanDuel Predicts no longer vote in the fair line. Same company or derived from other books, per your one-company rule.
  6. BoltOdds socket visibility. The key check and any "too many connections" refusal now show how many sockets are open on the key and the snapshot token budget.
  7. Clean hand-overs. At job end the engine waits for its sockets' close frames to leave, so the next runner isn't locked out by the old one's half-open sockets.
  8. Polymarket live prices. After Gamma lists the games, prices are re-read from the live CLOB order books (100 per request, free).
  9. PinnWire in one request. sport_id=all answers every sport at once; the scan's other sports read it from memory. Combined with yesterday's change (PinnWire only where SharpAPI lacks Pinnacle), the 100/day keys should last through the evening.
  10. Useless APIs rest themselves. An API that answers but matches no Novig game for 6 scans in a row is rested 1 h, then 3 h, then 9 h. It is never switched off for good on that ground.
  11. Correct "buy more" advice. Monthly runway is judged on credits used, not request count.
  12. Weekly docs mirror. api-docs.yml keeps every provider's docs and a change list in the repo.
  13. Memory files updated for Novig, BoltOdds, PropLine, Kalshi and Unabated, with the full study in research/api_full_audit_2026-10-22.md.

Found, queued next

  • Novig participants and candles: match props by player id instead of by name; Novig's own closing price for CLV studies.
  • Novig live first halves (NFL/NCAAF 1H now stay open live): not priced by the live lanes yet.
  • SharpAPI public betting splits: log for the studies.
  • BoltOdds now lists Circa and BookMaker: with "every book" on they flow in automatically; tomorrow's engine census will confirm.

Your calls

  • pinnapi: both keys are refused. Replace them or remove them in Settings › APIs.
  • Unabated: recommend switching it off; its free tier carries only books other feeds already give. The engine rests it on its own either way.
  • PropLine Streaming Lite ($39/mo) would push every price change instead of polling. Not needed now; only worth it if PropLine props become a major source of bets.

Follow-up, v1.0.52

  • Live result of v1.0.50 on the engine: the scan took 269 s (was 422 s), and all 12,610 Novig prices were screened without one-by-one book reads. The slowest step is now SharpAPI's first load on a fresh runner.
  • New research journals on the engine: Kalshi live scores of every game in play, SharpAPI public betting splits (DraftKings, Circa, BetMGM), and Novig's own closing price for every side the engine bet or bid on. All are read-only.
  • Not built yet: live first halves need period support through the whole live pricing path, so the first step is to measure whether SharpAPI's live feed carries them. Matching props by Novig player id waits until there's a measured matching failure to fix.

Buying streaming

  • ParlayAPI Business ($40/mo): don't buy. It fed 922 lines and none that no other feed had. 61% of your Pro credits are still unspent, and ParlayAPI's own docs say its data refreshes every 30-60 s upstream, so a socket wouldn't be much fresher than polling. SharpAPI's stream and BoltOdds already push those books.
  • PropLine Streaming Lite ($39/mo): not yet. PropLine is the only source for about 1,800 lines (mostly props), and its quotes run about 4 minutes old, so a push feed could help. But the bulk-props change means the 25,000-request Pro plan is enough now. Its +EV prop Overs also lost in the 25,909-bet test. Decide after a day or two, once we know whether bets on PropLine-only lines beat the close.

How to judge it tomorrow: the engine log's scan report (Novig seconds per scan), PinnWire keys left at midnight UTC, PropLine used vs its day share, the governor resting Unabated, and BoltOdds' props socket staying connected.
