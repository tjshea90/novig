## 1. The recipes page: what is new and what it gave us
Only one block is new since 2026-10-25: **"Record a live game"** (15c-15e): pregame props with `player_id`, a websocket subscription to `stat_update` + `play` + `game_state` for one event, the final box score and graded props, plus the open-source recorder and a full sample game.
- **Used at once:** the sample game is REAL `game_state` data. Replaying it through `PropLineGameState` found two bugs that the docs' description hid: `event_id` is a NUMBER in these frames (the parser would have dropped every one) and a game's status stays `in_progress` through its last play ("Game End"). Both fixed; six real frames are now a test fixture (`PropLineGameStateTest`).
- **Measured on it (what the docs do not say):** a `game_state` arrives a median **18 s** (p90 47 s) after the league's own play it reports; a `play` median 24 s (p90 224 s); a delivery comes every 15 s in play (p90 33 s, longest 433 s = halftime). So PropLine's live feed is a delayed, ESPN-like score, NOT a trigger for the seconds after a score: right for the live lane's context and for recording, wrong for score-chasing. (The docs say "read about every 5 seconds": that is their read rate, not our delay.)
- **Built from it:** socket 2 now also carries `stat_update`; `game_state` and `stat_update` are journaled with the steam/resolution/ev pushes (`propline-push-<day>`, archived with the engine's other journals). That is the live stat timeline PropLine says it does not keep ("record a game from the stream while it is live"), collected every night with no extra connection. The recorder repo itself is not needed (the engine's socket is the recorder).
- The other recipes (+EV per event, slate +EV, line shopping, CLV with `ev_vs_close_pct`, hit rate, arbitrage, reduced-juice props, DFS lines, periods, webhooks, Discord, futures, exports, native ids, freshness alerts) were already built or are listed below. One worth copying: **"read `ev_vs_close_pct`, not `clv_pct`"** (the recipe: price-vs-price CLV is vig-blind and flatters a bet on the juicy side: +6.52% vs +0.08% on one real bet). `tools/propline_clv.py` already devigs the close; `/v1/clv/grade` (used by `propline_grade_job.sh`) returns both: the grade job should publish `ev_vs_close_pct` as its headline. Noted for the next touch of that script.

## 3. The trial (what runs, how to read it)
`propline.yml` task `trial` (`tools/propline.py trial`, the engine's `PROPLINE_API_KEY` on the runner, read-only, about 60 requests): picks tonight's Novig moneylines in one league, prices up to 12 two-leg parlays from different games with `/v1/parlay/price`, one same-game slip with `bookmaker=all`, one `ev/calc`, Kalshi/Polymarket depth history for one game, `bets/settle` on a finished game with five bets whose answers are known (Over total-0.5 won, Under lost, Over at the total push, the two quarter lines half won), `ids/lookup`, grand salami, NHL daily goals, referees.
