## 1. Plumbing leak (class A, fixed): the engine never auto-bet totals or team totals
`CloudEngine.sendTrades` handed the trader the phone's RAW settings. The phone's `autoBetKinds` is the slider's old ladder (PROP, MONEYLINE, SPREAD); the slider at level 61 (`BetSlider.kinds`) also has
TOTAL and TEAM_TOTAL, and `forCloud` re-derives them, but only the scan got `forCloud`. So the 2026-10-17 change "totals win, the engine did not take totals: fixed the same day" reached the scan and never the
trader. Now the trader judges under `CloudEngine.forTrading(s)` (the same kinds as the scan). Test: `LiveBoardTest` (the engine's auto-bet judges with the bet slider's kinds).
Evidence that the kinds it now takes are good: team totals, the engine's own bid fills +77.5% ROI on 22 settled (t 4.2), the dataset's +EV team-total lines +41% ROI on 35 results (t 2.5), the 14:04Z digest
z +2.95; totals: dataset CLV +0.4% (n 67 closes), >= 6 h CLV +1.2% (t 1.5). No cap, stake, edge floor or guard changed.
Revert: in `sendTrades` pass `settings` instead of `forTrading(settings)`.
