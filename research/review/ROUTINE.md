## A missed slot is covered by the next account that is up (Tj, 2026-10-15: "usage will soon run out on one or two accounts: default to the next available account, or skip a session when it isn't available")
After your own duty, run `python3 -I tools/rotation.py missed`. It lists slots of the last 30 h that need covering: **NO-SHOW** (the account left no board note within 5 h of its time: usage cap, no repo, a dead routine) and **INTERRUPTED** (it checked in but never posted its finished-duty note: cut off mid-task by its usage cap; only when its duty was ANALYST or ENGINEER) that nobody has covered yet.
- **Cover up to TWO, oldest first: the first with that slot's WHOLE duty** (ANALYST: the digest analysis under its skip rule; ENGINEER: the top backlog item, and for INTERRUPTED read CHECKPOINT.md and the board first and finish what was started), **the second with its WATCH checks only.** The usual gates, skip rules and budgets still apply (an empty cover is fine). Then for each: `python3 -I tools/rotation.py cover <slot> --date <date>` so no other account repeats it.
- **An account that is out of usage simply does not run** (its routine fails to start or is cut off): that is the skip. Nothing needs to be told to anyone: the next slot's account sees the NO-SHOW/INTERRUPTED line and covers it. ## Each run
1. Do NOT run `bash tools/install-hooks.sh` in a routine session (it edits the user settings file and the permission classifier refuses it as persistence; with the repo attached the hooks already load from `.claude/settings.json`). Read CHECKPOINT.md and the board (`bash tools/coord.sh board 5`). If another session left something half done, finish that first.
2. **WATCH (every duty does this first, it is cheap):** engine health from `cloud-feed` (`status.json`, `engine-log.txt` through the contents API): heartbeat age (the engine runs 08:00-22:00 ET only: outside that window "idle" is correct), `novigRoute`, scan time, `problem`, the trading state and any stand-down (`GEOLOCATION_*` means Tj must open the Novig app: say so in the handoff note and push), the digest's age (older than 36 h: the workflow failed, read its run and fix it). Anything broken and fixable is class A.
3. **ANALYST:** from `git show origin/review-digest:latest.txt` (SUMMARY, SPLITS, the placed-bets and engine tables) and yesterday's digest: which groups (league, kind, lead time, books agree, fair age, price band, source, stake size, bid vs bet, fill rate) have CLV and ROI that are positive AND supported? Say n and the 95% interval and the t-statistic. Write `research/review/<date>.md` (short) and one line in `research/lab/NOTES.md`. Update `research/review/STATE.json`.
4. **ENGINEER:** the top open item of TASKS.md's AUTONOMY BACKLOG (or any class A idea the WATCH or ANALYST notes name): speed, accuracy, code quality, the engine's reliability. Ship through the full gate (`bash ship.sh`, `release.yml`, `record-release.sh`).
5. **Changes, by class:**
   - **A. Ship at once** (gate: the full test floor): bug fixes, speed, crashes, diagnostics, reports, the engine's plumbing: anything that does not change which bets or bids are placed, how they are priced or sized.
   - **B. Rules: allowed without asking Tj (Tj, 2026-10-15, explicitly: the trap guard hours, the kinds the auto-bet places, the books-agree counts and every other filter or setting may be changed when the data shows it too strict or unnecessary for profit; volume of fills counts as a goal beside edge, CLAUDE.md "profit AND volume") (his word of 2026-10-15), when the data and research are SOUND.** B is any change to bet or bid selection, fair-line haircuts, tiers, EV or star thresholds, Kelly fraction and stake caps, how much of the subaccount wallet is used (up to 100%), which kinds and sports are bet or bid, and the defaults of those switches. Sound means every gate holds:
     1. *Evidence twice:* the same finding in two digests with different data windows (the second adds at least a day of new games), 200+ settled bets (200+ closes for a CLV rule), a 95% interval (counts games) that excludes zero in the direction of the change, an effect of at least 1 point of ROI or CLV. State n, the interval and t. One look at many splits finds a t of 2 by luck: prefer a group the digest names by its own order or a prior reason over the best of many.
     2. *Loosening, adding a type or raising volume or size also needs* 400+ settled bets in the group and a week of the engine's own results (paper or live) agreeing with the digest. Tightening or removing a type that loses needs only gate 1.
     3. *Bounded:* one parameter family per change, at most 25% relative change of any one number, at most ONE class B change a day, behind a switch, with a test, through the full gate. Kelly stays fractional: the stake fraction may be raised only in steps of a quarter of its value, only after the engine's results at the current fraction are in.
     4. *Written down before it ships:* a line in `research/review/CHANGES.md` (date, what, evidence, the metric watched, and the REVERT RULE: "revert if the next two digests show the changed group's CLV below zero, or ROI t below -2 with n 100+"). A revert is class A: do it the day a revert rule fires.
     5. If the gates are not met, do NOT change it; write the idea and what evidence would settle it under `WATCH LIST` in TASKS.md and let the data grow. (Not "ask Tj": he has delegated the decision to the evidence.)
   - **C. Never, whatever the evidence:** put a key or token in a file or a note; use or ask for the Novig MANAGEMENT key anywhere but the phone; move money out of the Vigilant subaccount; open Pinnodds/BoltOdds/Kalshi sockets from a session; touch `mgm/`; change the signing keystore or applicationId; place or cancel an order from a session (only the engine's code does); rewrite `phone-data`; disable a kill switch, the engine-hours window, a stand-down on Novig's location or device checks (`GEOLOCATION_*`, address refusals), or the rule that an order is never sent twice; disable a workflow to hide a failure; run more than one Claude session at a time.
6. A change that touches the phone app is released as an APK; a change that is engine-only reaches the runner by itself at the engine's next start (the engine starts every morning at 08:00 ET). Say which in the checkpoint.

## The volume loop: profit first, but an engine that places 2 bets a day is not working (Tj, 2026-10-15: "So far the auto bet has only placed two bets. There must be much more volume ... a balance. The goal is always profit.")
EVERY ANALYST or ENGINEER run does this, in this order, and records the numbers in `research/review/CHANGES.md` (one line, even when nothing changes):
1. **Count**: engine orders and fills in the last 24 h (`trades.json` on `cloud-feed`), bets listed per scan (`status.rows`), and the held-back reasons (`status.tradeNote` now; `tradepass-<day>.jsonl` in the `cloud-archive-*` Release for the day). Target on a normal slate: tens of orders a day (a bet every few scans), not 2.
2. **Name the top blocker**: the biggest held-back reason by count. As of 2026-10-15 23:40Z: the 6-hour trap guard (34 of 41 listed), then the kinds list (team totals), then the edge floor (slider 61 = 2.9%) and stale other-book prices. Plumbing blockers (stale price, a feed erroring, BoltOdds 429, rate gates, a cap that cannot be reached) are fixed at once, no evidence gate: they are bugs.
3. **Rule blockers need the class B evidence, so produce it**: from the digest, the CLV (and ROI when n allows) of the bets THAT rule held back, by the rule's own bands (lead time, edge band, kind, books agreeing), inside the windows that are proven (<6 h before the start: CLV +2.0 to +2.9%). Loosen one rule one 25% step when the held-back bands beat the close with an interval above zero; tighten the same way when a band loses. One change a day, with a test and a revert rule.
4. **Raise volume without waiting on a rule**: the best volume lever that needs no loosening is more bets INSIDE the proven window (games 0-6 h out arrive all day; make sure each scan's list reaches the trader fresh, that stakes are not so small a fill is pointless, that the wallet-use cap is not what stops ordering, that two sides of one game are not blocking each other).
5. **Improve quality continuously**: re-rank bet types by CLV each week (profit-proven types first when sizes compete for the wallet), drop a type that loses with an interval below zero, and say what you changed to Tj with `tell_tj.py say`.
6. **The engine's BID desk is the lever for games the trap guard holds back (TCL31, 2026-10-16)**: taker bets are limited to the last 6 h before the start (proven), so everything further out earns by RESTING BIDS under the fair, which the engine's desk (`CloudBidRunner`: the phone's bid desk, unchanged) posts all day while engine trading is LIVE and `gates.cloudBids` is on. Every run also counts bids: posted / resting / filled per day and the fill rate (`status.deskNote`, `desk.json` on `cloud-feed`, bid fills are `TradeEntry`s whose note starts `bid` in `trades.json`, graded by the same grader), judges bid fills against the close like bets (CLV by BidTier: proven / steady / risky), and tunes the BID rules (slider `bidLevel`, `bidMany`, deploy %, kinds, margin) with the same class B gates. Zero bids resting during the engine's hours with candidates available is a bug to find (the candidate list in the log, `deskStopped`, the picked-off guard halt, the wallet), not a result.
7. **The flat datasets are the analysis input (TCL33, 2026-10-16)**: `engine-grade.yml` publishes `lines-<day>.csv.gz` (EVERY bet the engine's scan saw within 2 points of break-even, shown or not, with odds, lead time, EV, books agreeing, sharp agreement, stars, close, CLV, result) and `bids.csv.gz` (every bid's whole life) to the `engine-dataset` Release with `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` (`python3 -I tools/lines_dataset.py dictionary`). Download them (`curl -L https://github.com/tjshea90/novig/releases/download/engine-dataset/<file>`), judge rules by CLV first (many more rows than results), then ROI with t-statistics, and read bid tables with `python3 -I tools/bid_summary.py bids.csv.gz` (fill rate and fill_ev by kind, hours to start, margin, rung). Near-misses (`shown`=0 with `not_shown_why` "edge under the minimum") are the evidence for lowering a floor; `shown`=1 rows with negative CLV are the evidence for raising one.
The balance rule: never lower the edge floor below what the data says still beats the close, never remove +EV at the real price; volume comes from more good bets, not worse ones.
