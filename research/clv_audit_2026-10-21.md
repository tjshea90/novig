# CLV audit of the engine's listed bets (2026-10-21, CH56)

Source: `engine-dataset` Release `lines-*.csv.gz` (13,103 lines). Listed bets (shown=1): 612; settled 373; **only 164 of the 612 have any CLV at all** (27%).

| close source | listed bets with CLV | mean CLV (se) | ROI per unit staked (n) |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Novig's last trades | 86 | -0.56% (0.73) | -18.2% (81) |
| SGO Pinnacle close | 47 | +0.78% (1.04) | -10.0% (46) |
| SGO Circa close | 11 | +3.17% (1.26) | -5.5% (11) |
| ESPN DraftKings close | 20 | +2.63% (1.50) | +45.4% (18) |

Listed and settled: ROI +0.7% per unit, standard error 5.5% (373 bets), against a claimed edge of +3.4%.

Findings:
- CLV is missing for 73% of listed bets, and the sources disagree in kind: "Novig's last trades" (a handful of trades, often 3-10) is the noisiest and is the source with the worst numbers; the sharp closes (Pinnacle, Circa) are the ones to trust.
- With SE 5.5% on ROI, 373 bets cannot tell +3% from 0%: "beating CLV but losing money" is mostly sample size. The CLV numbers themselves (n 11 to 86 per source) are too thin to call either way.
- Method note: close sources are mixed per bet (`close_via`), so one overall CLV number blends a noisy and a sharp source. Report CLV per source, as above.
