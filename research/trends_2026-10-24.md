# Stats and trends beyond CLV/EV: do they find profitable bets? (TR, 2026-10-24)

Tj: "Can we get any valuable information from the historical data ... besides clv and EV? Maybe make a system that finds profitable bets based on stats or trends?"

**Verdict: a player's recent form carries a little information Novig's close misses (about 1 point), the market already moves toward it, and it is far too small to beat Novig's taker vig on props. It is not a stand-alone profit system. It is worth at most a small tilt on the bid desk's fair (maker, no vig), and only after the bids themselves are proven on Novig's close (SB2b).**

## Data and method
- PropLine's resolved archive of Novig (Releases `propline-archive-2026-10-16..23`, 637,338 graded outcomes, games 2026-05-23..10-23). Props paired Over/Under at one line with both closing prices: 156,630; with 5+ earlier games of the same player and stat in the archive: 96,575.
- Signals, from games BEFORE the one priced: `gap` = (player's mean of the stat over his last up to 10 games - the line) / his standard deviation; `hit` = how often he went over that game's main line; `hitx` = hit rate minus what the prices then implied; steam = open-to-close move of 5+ points.
- Yardstick: the result minus Novig's devigged close (multiplicative), and ROI at Novig's closing price; counted by GAME (game-clustered errors). Fit on May-Aug, judged on Sep-Oct (held out).
- Scripts: `research/trends_2026-10-24/` (`load.py` -> `trend.py` -> `tilt.py`, `open.py`).

## Findings
1. **Recent form is a real but tiny signal.** Correlation of `gap` with the result beyond the close: r +0.013 (z 3.1) on May-Aug, +0.017 (z 3.5) on Sep-Oct. As a fair-value tilt fitted on May-Aug it improves Sep-Oct log loss by 0.0002 (95% CI by game -0.0004 .. +0.00002: touches zero) and moves the fair 0.8 points on average (1.6 at the 90th percentile). `hit` and `hitx` are weaker and did not survive as a tilt.
2. **The market already prices most of it.** `gap` predicts Novig's open-to-close move strongly (r +0.07, z 15-16 in both halves) but by only 0.1-0.3 points: by the close the trend is in the price.
3. **No trend bucket made money at Novig's taker prices.** Best held-out buckets: Over when the player's average is 0.5 sd above the line, -3.8% ROI (beat the close +1.1 pts); Under when 0.5 sd below, -2.8% (+1.2 pts). All Overs: -8.5%, all Unders -4.6%. Novig's prop vig is bigger than the signal.
4. **Steam does not repeat.** May-Aug: a line steamed 5+ points toward Over then lost to the close by 4.4 points (fade it); Sep-Oct: +1.4 the other way. Same as RESEARCH.md §72 (following or fading steam is not an edge).
5. **What already works is already in the stars:** the Over/Under bias (Overs lose beyond the close: -1.65 pts May-Aug, -0.50 Sep-Oct) and the favourite-longshot bias are type cells of `star_evidence.json` (SX).

## Use
- TR3 (a `gap` tilt on prop fair values) is NOT built: it fails the ROUTINE.md class B bar (the held-out log-loss interval includes zero) and on the taker side it cannot turn a losing bet into a winner. Revisit as a BID-desk tilt for MLB/NHL props once SB2b shows the bids beat Novig's close and another month of archive confirms finding 1.
