## Follow-up (Tj: more free PinnWire/pinnapi/PropLine keys? PropLine Pro vs 25 free keys? SharpAPI?)
- PropLine: 25 free keys = 25,000 requests/day, same as one Pro key, for $0 and the SAME freshness (vendor: every tier has the same data freshness). Pro/Hobby add the analytics free keys redact: /odds/closing, /movement (steam), /ev, /best-line prices, CLV grading. The app does not use those; it needs raw odds (free). Key rotation exists (KeyPool). Free keys are therefore equal or better for scanning; paying only buys the redacted analytics and pushes (Lite). Caveat: check PropLine's terms before farming keys (a vendor can ban pooled free accounts; not verified), and the three keys in use are already working.
- PinnWire/pinnapi: more free keys = 100 requests/day each (20/min), one request per sport returns every Pinnacle line: 9 keys = 900/day; 20 keys = 2,000/day, enough for ~10 scans a sport-set a day each; real-time Pinnacle. Free keys are the best sharp source per dollar.
- SharpAPI (sharpapi.io; vendor claims): Free 12 req/min, only DraftKings+FanDuel, 60 s delay; Hobby $79 (5 books, 120/min), Pro $229 (15 books), Sharp $399 (25 books, "sharp sportsbooks"); WebSocket/SSE add-on $99. Pinnacle is not stated on any tier except the vague "sharp sportsbooks" at $399. Not worth it: more expensive than PropLine/Pinnodds, Pinnacle access unproven, no latency figures. Ask sales@sharpapi.io if Pinnacle is on Hobby before ever paying.
