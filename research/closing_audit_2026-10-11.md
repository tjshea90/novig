# Closing odds and CLV audit (2026-10-11)

Read-only audit of how Vigilant computes closing odds and CLV, requested because Tj asked that the closing odds in the bet export be accurate. Two of four lenses finished before the workflow was stopped for usage reasons (lenses `sources` and `export` did not): the findings below are UNVERIFIED by a second reviewer (the adversarial verify stage never ran) and must be traced in code before being acted on. Each carries file, line, scenario and suggested fix.

## A CNO/ParlayAPI read is dated by when it was fetched, not by how old its data was, so a stale page passes as a fresh true close
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/BetRecheck.kt`:461  severity: wrong-number
- what: changeFor() writes closingSeenAtMs = minOf(now, view.fetchedAtMs). fetchedAtMs is the HTTP time. The view also carries CnoBooksView.dataAtMs (fetchedAtMs minus CNO's 'Last Updated' age; CnoClient.kt:161-162 fills it) and, for ParlayAPI views, a per-book CnoBookPrice.atMs (ParlayBooks.kt:138), and both are ignored. CNO only treats a page as stuck after 10 min (CnoChecks.STUCK_MS) and its owner quotes 1-2 min latency (RESEARCH.md §113). The comment on line 460 says 'a cached page can't pass for a close', but only the app's own cache is handled. RESEARCH.md:5721 lists the re-check as known to be aged by read time. CnoMakerLines.pageAt (line 82) already does this correctly for bids. Because the 15-min rule, the 12-min inWindow rule and the 3-min needsFinalRead rule all key off closingSeenAtMs, the error also stops the capture from retrying.
- failure: At T-2 min the capture reads a CNO game page whose 'Last Updated' says 9 min ago. closingSeenAtMs = T-2 min, so needsFinalRead() is false (age under 3 min) and captured() accepts it. The 'close' is really the line from T-11 min. A move in the last 10 minutes (lineup news, a prop pulled) is missed. Because supersedes() compares the fetch-dated stamp, this stale value also replaces a genuinely fresher Vigilant-derived close. If the page carries no age, cnoAgeSeconds is null and the read counts as 0 s old (fresh).
- fix: In changeFor use the data time: seen = minOf(now, view.dataAtMs), then the oldest of the view's prices' atMs when they exist. When cnoAgeSeconds is null, subtract a pessimistic default as CnoMakerLines does (UNKNOWN_PAGE_AGE_MS) or do not count it as a close. Export the data time with the close.

## The documented 'average of both reads' close almost never lands: CNO is dated by fetch time and Vigilant by its oldest book price, so the merge loses to CNO alone
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/BetTracker.kt`:838  severity: misleading
- what: In ClosingCapture the CNO read runs first. changeFor writes closingFair = CNO's fair with closingSeenAtMs = its fetch time F. mergeReads then writes the blend (c+v)/2 only if ClosingLine.supersedes(b, seen), and seen = minOf(cnoAtMs, vigAsOfMs ?: vigAtMs) (lines 845-851). vigAsOfMs is the OLDEST book price in Vigilant's fair (applyFair, line 786), which is normally older than a page fetched just now, so seen < F and the blend is rejected (ClosingLine.kt:133). nowFair, nowEv and nowVia = 'both' are still overwritten by the blend, so the bet reports 'both' while its close is CNO alone. The two reads are dated on different scales, one optimistic and one pessimistic, which makes CNO systematically win. BothReadsTest.kt:59-100 passes only because it gives both reads equal timestamps and no vigAsOfMs. Lines 270-279 of the same test show the fetch-dated CNO close surviving.
- failure: A CNO prop bet is read at T-110 s with cnoFair 0.42 and Vigilant's fair 0.46 (oldest book 4 min old). The stored close is 0.42, the app shows nowVia=both, and the exported record says the close is the average. The close is CNO's own soft consensus, the same method as fairAtBet, which is the circularity in RESEARCH.md §109. An exporter that infers the close's basis from nowVia mislabels it.
- fix: Date both reads on one scale (data time per finding 1). Either record the close's basis explicitly (closeBasis = cno | vigilant | both | history) or let a merged read replace a single-source close when its own newest/oldest input is no older than the held one. Add a test with a realistic vigAsOfMs.

## startsTs is frozen at logging and the capture ends at the scheduled start, so late or early starts get a wrong close
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/ClosingLine.kt`:63  severity: misleading
- what: No code path reassigns TrackedBet.startsTs after the bet is logged (only the constructors at BetTracker.kt:367, 470, 525, 615, 911, 942). Everything uses that scheduled time: closeOf needs now >= startsTs, a read counts as pregame when now < startsTs (BetTracker.kt:798, 841; BetRecheck.kt:454), the last capture try is 60 s before it (LAST_TRY_MS), and NovigTradeCloses takes trades in [startsTs-30min, startsTs) (HistoricalCloses.kt:273). Books close at the real start, so (a) a game that starts late (NBA/NHL/MLB usually a few minutes, rain delays, tennis order of play) is frozen as 'final' at about T-2 min while the pregame market keeps moving, and every read after the scheduled start is discarded as 'odds now'; (b) a game that starts early (tennis) has its last reads, including the alarm reads at T-6 to T-1 min, taken on in-play odds and stamped as the close. The code knows tennis starts are loose (BetGrader TENNIS_START_GAP_MS 24 h; ParlayBooks uses league.maxStartGapHours) but not here. Op4Closes uses OddsPapi's trueStartMs and OpCloses uses OddsPapi's startMs, so history closes and captured closes are measured to different instants.
- failure: A tennis match scheduled 14:00 starts at 13:25. The 13:54 and 13:58 captures read live odds (one player already up a break) and write them as closingFair with seen < startsTs, so a bet shows 'beat the close' or '-20% CLV' on an in-play price. Or an MLB game listed 19:05 throws its first pitch at 19:40 after a delay. The close is the 19:03 line, and pitcher or weather news priced in the next 35 min is ignored, and the Pinnacle history close for the same bet is never even asked for because captured() wins (finding 5).
- fix: Refresh startsTs (or keep a separate liveStartsTs) from the Novig event or the CNO row on each capture, and treat a game as started when ESPN or the book says it is in progress. Reject reads whose game state is not pregame. Export both the logged and the current start, and the close's age before the real start.

## Novig-trades 'close' is a 30-minute VWAP that includes the bettor's own fills and may rest on one tiny trade
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/HistoricalCloses.kt`:281  severity: wrong-number
- what: NovigTradeCloses averages every STRAIGHT trade on the bet's outcome in [start-30min, start) by volume (WINDOW_MS line 386, windowMs line 231). That is not the app's own 15-minute close rule (ClosingLine.TRUE_CLOSE_MS), and on average it is about 15 min stale relative to the start. Trades are anonymised (NOVIG_API.md:325), and parse()/closes() never exclude Tj's own fills (fillIds are kept on the bet but unused here) or his other Vigilant bids hit on the same outcome. There is no minimum trade count: Found is returned for mine.size == 1 (the via string just says 'last trades (1)'). RESEARCH.md §113 itself says only closes with 3 or more trades should be trusted. Windows that cross midnight Eastern are read from one day file only (line 260 groups by etDate(startsTs)), so a game starting 00:00-00:30 ET loses the pre-midnight part or gets 'no trades' (final).
- failure: A bid on a thin NHL shots prop fills at 0.455 at T-20 min and is the only trade in the window. The close is 0.455 = the bid's own price, so CLV is exactly -fee/cost ('did not beat the close') regardless of the real market. With 6 trades in the window the VWAP of a line that drifted 0.46 to 0.50 reads 0.47 instead of the true last 0.50, understating CLV by about 6%, and the own fill drags it further toward the bet's price.
- fix: Use a window of at most 5-10 min, or the last trade (or a time-weighted last few). Subtract the bettor's own fills, matching on timestamp and size, or at least flag those bets. Require at least 3 trades, else return None. Read the previous ET day's file when start-window crosses midnight. Export trade count and last-trade age.

## A captured close up to 15 min old permanently blocks the independent Pinnacle/ESPN/Novig history close
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/HistoricalCloses.kt`:513  severity: misleading
- what: closeOf() returns captured(b) first (ClosingLine.kt:64) and CloseBackfill.due()/reopened() only look for bets where ClosingLine.closeOf(b, now) == null (lines 508 and 513). Any read in the last 15 minutes before the start, including a 6-14 min old one when the final read failed or when SGO-mode quotes were 3-10 min old, is therefore final. The sharper history source (ParlayAPI Pinnacle at the start, SGO, OddsPapi) is never asked, and no other close is stored for comparison. For CNO bets the kept value is also the circular CNO re-read (RESEARCH.md §109: 79% of Tracker closes).
- failure: A player prop bet is read once at T-12 min (the next try fails offline), so seen = T-12 min and captured() counts it. After the game the backfill skips it, and the export shows a CNO-only soft-consensus value from 12 min before the start as 'the closing odds', while Pinnacle's actual close was available for 1 credit.
- fix: Keep captured and history closes in separate fields and ask the history sources for every started bet whose captured close is older than about 3 min or is CNO-only. Choose the close by recency and independence (Pinnacle history, then fresh capture), and export all candidates plus the one chosen.

## No check that the close was read after the bet, so a close can be the bet's own placement-time line
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/ClosingLine.kt`:70  severity: edge-case
- what: captured() requires only seen < startsTs and startsTs - seen <= 15 min. Nothing compares seen with createdAtMs. In the auto-scan an auto-bet is placed and then captureClosing() runs in the same cycle (AutoScan.kt:256-270). The new bet has nowAtMs == null, so BetRecheck.kt:337 reads it at once, and observe() (BetTracker.kt:707) can write the scan fair the bet was placed on. For a bet placed in its last 15 min, that read, taken seconds after the placement from the same books and devig as fairAtBet, becomes closingFair. A later read normally replaces it, but a bet placed in the last ~3 min is never re-read (needsFinalRead needs age > 3 min, LAST_TRY_MS is 60 s), and neither is one whose later reads fail. In the 'Novig only' lens, NovigNow.apply stores the latest pre-start ask as novigClose, which is the ask the taker just paid. RESEARCH.md:2825 even documents 'closes at the line it was bet at' for this case, contradicting the ClosingLine.kt header ('never closed at the line it was bet at').
- failure: Auto-bet fires at T-100 s from a +3% EV scan. The same cycle's captureClosing reads the CNO page, giving closingFair equal to the fair behind the bet. CLV is about +3%, a 'beat the close' by construction, and these late bets feed ClvStats.beat and the 'under 30 min' lead split.
- fix: Count a close only if closingSeenAtMs >= createdAtMs + a margin (e.g. 60 s) and otherwise leave it null (history backfill then supplies a real one). At minimum export closeReadAfterBetSec and flag closes with a gap under 60 s as not independent.

## BetLedger rows (the base of the all-bets export) carry no close time, mislabel the close source and export an untimed novigClose
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/BetLedger.kt`:71  severity: misleading
- what: Row.closeVia is set from b.closeVia, but a captured close has closeVia == null (only history closes set it), so with explicitNulls=false the source is simply absent for every captured close, and the Row has no closingSeenAtMs. A reader cannot tell a 2-min-old read from a 14-min-old one, or captured from history. StudyExport.kt:166 does map SOURCE_CAPTURED, so the two exports disagree. closeFinal here is the backfill's 'stop looking' flag, not 'the close is final'. novigClose is exported raw without novigCloseAtMs, and NovigNow.apply keeps the latest pre-start Novig ask however old (lines 36-42, no 15-min rule; only NovigNow.view applies captured()), so it can be a read from T-9 h. It is also Novig's ask (the taker's price), which for a resting bid overstates the close by the spread. No export field says how the CNO/Vigilant blend was formed (finding 2).
- failure: Claude analyses the export and averages 'CLV' across bets whose closes range from 1 min to 15 min before the start, from CNO-only reads and from Pinnacle history, with no way to filter. novigClose of 0.52 read at 13:00 for a 22:00 game looks like the market at the start.
- fix: Add per bet: closeSource from ClosingLine.closeOf (not b.closeVia), closeSeenAtMs, closeAgeBeforeStartSec, closeReadAfterBetSec, closeBasis (cno | vigilant | both | history), novigCloseAtMs, trade count for Novig-trades closes, startsAtMs as logged and as currently known. Apply the 15-min rule to novigClose in the export, or null it.

## History closes lack a recency guard (except ParlayAPI's) and the plausibility check is 10 points wide for bets placed within an hour
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/ClosePlausibility.kt`:20  severity: edge-case
- what: maxMove(gap <= 1 h) is 0.10 probability, from a measured maximum of 2.6 points. For a bet placed minutes before the start, the close is within a couple of points of fairAtBet, so a wrong-side or wrong-game close up to 10 points away passes (any 40-60% line). Beyond that, only ParlayCloses enforces that a price is near the start (tooEarly, 2 h, line 247). SgoCloses accepts closeOdds with no timestamp (lines 112-114, unverified against a live key). OpCloses/Op4Closes take the last active tick at or before the start with no age cap (OpCloses.kt:144, Op4Closes.kt:103), and OpCloses accepts a clv price stamped up to GRACE_MS = 2 min after the scheduled start (OpCloses.kt:139, 160), which can be a live price. ParlayCloses.parseGameLine's Odds-API-shaped branch (line 392) has no timing check either.
- failure: A prop that SGO or OddsPapi stopped quoting at T-6 h still returns its last active price as the 'close'. A prop placed 10 min before the start has its true close 1 point from fairAtBet, but a 9-point-off opposite side is accepted as the close.
- fix: Tighten maxMove for gaps under 30 min (e.g. 0.04) and add a tooEarly-style cap (about 2 h, or less for the game lines) to SGO/OddsPapi/OddsPapi Free. Use a strict at-or-before-start cut-off for OddsPapi's clv price.

### Lens notes
Read-only audit of the timing and 'what counts as a true close' lens. No files were edited and Gradle was not run. Files read: ClosingLine, ClosePlausibility, HistoricalCloses, BetTracker (observe, applyFair, mergeReads, stats), BetRecheck, SgoCloses, OpCloses, Op4Closes, ParlayCloses, NovigNow, BetLedger, ClosingCapture, plus AutoScan's closing step, BetInsight, CnoClient/CnoModels, and RESEARCH.md §41, §109, §113.

Sound in this lens:
- captured() is strict: the read must be before the start and no more than 15 min earlier.
- closeOf() returns nothing before the start, for bets placed at or after the start (live, including maker fills, since Novig cancels resting bids at the start: MakerQuote.kt:555) and for locks.
- supersedes() is monotonic in the read's age, so an older cached read cannot replace a fresher one.
- The retry, final-read and alarm arithmetic (LEAD 6 min, DUE 12 min, RETRY 2 min, FINAL 110 s, LAST_TRY 60 s) is internally consistent, and a read that completes after the start is correctly discarded (BetRecheck.kt:454).
- Bets' stored closes never come from after the start; the Tracker sheet labels non-true closes 'CLV so far' or 'Vs last pregame read' (TrackerBetSheet.kt:335-336), and BetInsight.clv (clvPercent, no 15-min rule) is not used in any stat.
- ParlayCloses.tooEarly (2 h) and OpCloses's live-price replacement are good guards.
- Capture throughput is fine: CNO bulk pace is about 1 page per second, so a 100-bet kickoff slot finishes inside the 6-min lead.

Other points for the export design:
- The final read is scheduled at T-110 s. If Vigilant's bets-only pass lands after the start, mergeReads discards it, because its `at < startsTs` test uses the save time, not the data time (BetTracker.kt:841), and the earlier close stands. Pass duration was not measured here.
- The CLV definition for a CNO bet is circular: its fairAtBet and its close come from the same CNO consensus (RESEARCH §109). The export should carry closeBasis so Claude can restrict beat-the-close analysis to independent closes (Pinnacle history, fresh Vigilant/Pinnacle reads).
- SGO and OddsPapi closes have never run against a live key (CLAUDE.md), so their time semantics are unverified.

Most severe for Tj's 'make sure it is accurate': findings 1 and 4 (a stale line labelled fresh; a 30-min VWAP that includes his own trades), then 2, 3 and 5 (which line becomes the close and when it is taken). Finding 7 is the cheap fix inside the new export.

## History closes are devigged multiplicatively, but fair-at-bet and captured closes use POWER: CLV inflated 5-13% on underdogs
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/SgoCloses.kt`:114  severity: wrong-number
- what: Every after-the-fact close source calls Devig.multiplicative on the two sides (SgoCloses.kt:114, OpCloses.kt:112, Op4Closes.kt:94, ParlayCloses.kt:238, HistoricalCloses.kt:167). The bet's own fairAtBet and the captured close come from FairValue with ScanSettings.devigMethod = POWER (ScanSettings.kt:114) blended with market average, and CNO bets use CNO's worst-case devig. CLV = closeFair/cost-1 therefore compares two different fair-price definitions. Multiplicative devig puts too much probability on the longshot. I computed it for typical Pinnacle pairs: -150/+130 dog 0.4202 vs power 0.4160 (+1.0% rel); -250/+205 dog +3.9%; +260/-320 dog 0.2672 vs power 0.2530 (+5.6%); +450/-600 dog +12.8%; favourites are understated by 0.5-2.4%. Close sources are tried in the order SGO, OddsPapi v5, OddsPapi v4, ParlayAPI, ESPN, Novig trades (VigilantApp.kt:514), so most non-captured closes are multiplicative. The app's bets skew to +EV dogs and overs.
- failure: A +260 prop bet with +3% EV at bet (power fair) whose Pinnacle line did not move at all is back-filled with a multiplicative close: closeFair 0.2672 vs cost 0.2530 gives CLV +5.6% and 'beat the close', although the same market read by the app at T-2min would give about 0%. Average CLV and beat% are inflated by the mix of captured and back-filled closes, and differ by source (closeVia).
- fix: Devig back-filled closes with the same method as the app's fair (Devig.devig(raw, settings.method), POWER by default; CNO bets: CnoBooks' own method), or export both closeFair_mult and closeFair_power and compute CLV from one method consistently. At minimum export a closeDevig field and report CLV per closeVia in the summary. Do not average captured and back-filled CLV without a per-source split.

## closeReadMs/closeLeadSec for back-filled closes are the time of the lookup, giving negative 'seconds before the start'
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/BetHistory.kt`:214  severity: wrong-number
- what: closeReadMs = if captured then closingSeenAtMs else closeLookedAtMs. For every history close (SGO, OddsPapi, ParlayAPI, ESPN, Novig trades) closeLookedAtMs is when CloseBackfill ran, at least 10 minutes after the start and often days later (HistoricalCloses.kt:461, AFTER_START_MS). closeLeadSec = (startsMs - closeReadMs)/1000 (line 234) is then negative by minutes to days, and says nothing about how old the close price is. The field is the only freshness signal in the export.
- failure: Claude filters 'closeLeadSec <= 900' to keep true closes and silently drops every back-filled Pinnacle/ESPN close, or sorts them as 'read after the start' and concludes they are live prices. A Pinnacle prop price taken 100 minutes before the start (ParlayAPI accepts up to 2 h) has no lead value at all.
- fix: Emit closeReadMs/closeLeadSec only for SOURCE_CAPTURED (closingSeenAtMs). For history sources emit null, or the source's own snapshot time where it exists (ParlayAPI snapshot_time, OddsPapi closeMs/tick time). Add a separate closeLookedMs if wanted. Add closeTier = captured | history.

## Novig-trades close includes Tj's own fill and bid fills, is a 30-minute VWAP, and has no minimum trade count
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/HistoricalCloses.kt`:278  severity: wrong-number
- what: NovigTradeCloses takes every STRAIGHT single trade on the bet's outcome in [start-30min, start) and returns sum(cost)/sum(qty) (lines 272-283; parse at 370-380 does not look at the TAKER/MAKER 'side' column). The window contains the bet's own taker row, or a bid's own maker row, whenever it was placed or filled in the last 30 minutes (its fillIds are not in the CSV, nothing is excluded). It also mixes taker rows (ask side) and maker rows (bid side), so with one-sided flow the price sits at the ask or bid, not mid: RESEARCH.md section 42's own cross-check was ESPN-DK 0.6225 vs Novig 0.6337, 1.1 points (1.8% relative, the size of the CLV being measured). A 30-minute VWAP is on average about 15 minutes older than the close. There is no minimum of trades or volume (the label even reads 'Novig's last trades (1)').
- failure: Bet 100 contracts on a thin prop at 0.450 at T-12min; the only other trades in the last 30 min total 300 contracts at 0.470. The 'close' is (45+141)/400 = 0.465 instead of 0.470, so CLV shrinks from +4.4% to +3.3%. If the bet's own fill is the only trade, closeFair == cost (to 1e-16): CLV is exactly 0 and, with the strict >0 test, is a coin-flip 'beat' or 'miss'. Bids (maker rows at the bid price) are pulled toward their own price the same way.
- fix: Exclude trades matching the bet's own fills (same outcome, ts within a couple of seconds of createdAtMs, qty <= contracts); use only rows after the bet; require N>=3 trades or X contracts else None; weight to the last 5 minutes or take the last trade pair per side; and flag closeVia 'Novig trades' as low grade in the export (closeTier). Treat it as a Novig-internal price, not a market close.

## Three of the four paid close sources have no staleness bound; no source requires the close to be after the bet
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/OpCloses.kt`:139  severity: misleading
- what: A captured close must be within 15 minutes of the start (ClosingLine.TRUE_CLOSE_MS), and ParlayAPI rejects a snapshot more than 2 h early (ParlayCloses.kt:211, tooEarly, props and totals only; the moneyline and spread 'mine' side only). But OpCloses.closePrices accepts any clv row with closeMs <= start+2min and treats a null closeMs as fine (line 139); Op4Closes takes the last active Pinnacle tick at or before the start however old (line 103); SgoCloses.closeAt reads closeOdds with no timestamp at all (line 112). These are first, second and third in the source order (VigilantApp.kt:514). Pinnacle often pulls or limits props 30-120 minutes before kickoff. Also no source checks that the close's time is after the bet's createdAtMs. CLV against a snapshot older than the bet is not a close.
- failure: Pinnacle posts a player prop at T-6h and pulls it at T-90min; the last active tick (T-90min) becomes the 'close' for a bet placed at T-20min. CLV measures the bet price against a line that predates the bet by about 70 minutes and is labelled a true close. The exported closeLeadSec cannot reveal it (see the closeLeadSec finding).
- fix: Give every CloseSource.Found a snapshot time. Reject, or tier, closes older than 15 min (strict) / 2 h (loose) before the start, and always reject snapshot < createdAtMs. Export closeSnapshotLeadSec for every source and a closeTier. Verify SGO's closeOdds timestamp semantics against a live key (never run live; SPORTSGAMEODDS_API.md).

## novigCloseOdds is any pre-start read at any age, is an ask rather than a fair price, and inflates bid CLV in the Novig-only lens
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/BetHistory.kt`:235  severity: misleading
- what: TrackedBet.novigClose is set by NovigNow.apply for any read before the start that is newer than the last (NovigNow.kt:35-43), so it can be from hours earlier; there is no 15-minute rule and novigCloseAtMs is not exported. The value is Novig's best offer to buy the side (NovigNow.odds, line 24), including the spread, and the two sides' asks do not sum to 1. It is exported through fairOddsOf as if it were a fair probability. In the Novig-only lens (NovigNow.view, line 82) closingFair = that ask, so a maker bid (cost = bid price, below the ask) gets CLV = ask_close/bid - 1, which contains the whole spread and margin by construction.
- failure: A bid posted at 0.440 with Novig's ask at 0.465 at the close shows CLV +5.7% in the Novig-only + Bids view even if the market never moved. In the file, a novigCloseOdds taken at T-5h looks like a close, and Claude compares it with a fair-based closeOdds as if they were the same thing.
- fix: Export novigCloseAskOdds with novigCloseLeadSec and null it when the read is more than 15 min before the start. Do not compute bid CLV against an ask; for bids use the bid or mid. Document in the README that it is an ask, not fair.

## ClosePlausibility uses the fill-time gap with the posting-time fair for bids, so real adverse moves are dropped (survivorship flatters bid CLV)
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/tracker/ClosePlausibility.kt`:29  severity: edge-case
- what: gap = b.startsTs - b.createdAtMs, and for a bid createdAtMs is the first FILL's timestamp (BetTracker.kt:614) while fairAtBet is the fair at POSTING (BetTracker.kt:594/617, MakerDesk.target(): fair = fair at post, MakerDesk.kt:151-157). Bids rest for hours (12 h+ bids exist) and fill when the market moves against them. maxMove(gap) is chosen from the short fill gap (10 points inside an hour) but the move being tested spans post-to-close (up to 20-25 points allowed for the longer gap). The check runs on every back-filled close (HistoricalCloses.kt:451).
- failure: A bid posted 20 h before the start at fair 0.55 is filled 40 min before the start after the player is ruled out; Pinnacle's true close is 0.38 (17 points). maxMove(40min) = 0.10, so the close is rejected as 'probably another game or side', the bet has no CLV, and the worst bid outcomes are exactly the ones missing from bid CLV.
- fix: For bids use the posting gap (MakerBid.postedAtMs, or atBet from post) for the tolerance, or compare against the fair at fill (fairAtFill); never reject a close by a bound shorter than the age of the fair it is compared with. Keep a flag closeRejectedImplausible in the export instead of silently dropping.

## A bid's 'time before the game when placed' is the post time for single-fill bids but the first-fill time for multi-fill bids, while placed/createdAt is the fill time
- file: `data/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/data/novig/trading/maker/MakerDesk.kt`:856  severity: misleading
- what: recordFills calls tracker.logMakerFills(bid.target(), ...) at line 856 BEFORE it stores firstFillAtMs (line 862). On the first pass target() therefore builds the record with AtBets.bid(this, firstFillAtMs ?: postedAtMs) = postedAtMs (line 156), so atBet.minutesToStart is the lead at posting. When more fills arrive, withFills() replaces atBet (BetTracker.kt:595) from a target that now has firstFillAtMs, so the lead becomes the lead at first fill. ## EV and CLV ignore the push probability on integer lines (scale overstated by 1/(1-pPush))
- file: `engine/src/main/kotlin/com/tjshea/vigilant/engine/EvMath.kt`:23  severity: edge-case
- what: evPercent = fair/cost - 1 and CLV = closeFair/cost - 1 (ClosingLine.kt:91) assume win-or-lose, with fair the two-way devigged (conditional on no push) chance. - fix: Cosmetic for CLV (ratio is consistent); if exact EV matters, divide by 1/(1-pPush) using the line's push probability, or export isIntegerLine so Claude can discount it.

### Lens notes
Scope: read-only audit. Read ClosingLine, ClosePlausibility, HistoricalCloses (ESPN, Novig trades, CloseBackfill), BetTracker (stats, mergeReads, logApi, logMakerFills, setPrice, logAlert, cnoBet), BetRecheck, NovigNow, BetLedger, SgoCloses, OpCloses, Op4Closes, ParlayCloses, ClosingCapture, plus engine Fees/Odds/Devig/EvMath/FairValue, RESEARCH.md sections 41/42/79, MakerDesk, and the in-flight export code written during the audit (BetHistory.kt and BetDims.kt as of 15:31Z; they may change before you read this). No gradle was run.

Sound, checked:
- CLV = closeFair/cost - 1 is the same everywhere: ClosingLine.clv:91, BetTracker.stats (via ClosingLine.clv), BetLedger.row, TrackerBetSheet:169, TrackerScreen:715, Diagnostics.closeRows:644, StudyExport. It equals the EV the price paid had at the close, so it is the same as (decimal paid / decimal of fair close) - 1.
- Cost/fee: pregame game markets pay no taker fee (WHEN_LIVE, Fees.kt), so cost == price pregame. API bets: stake = paid+fee, price = paid/payout, cost = stake/payout from the summed fills (VWAP across partial fills); logMakerFills re-aggregates all fills of the order; makers pay no fee (cost = price). Live bets (createdAt >= start) and locks never get a CLV (closeOf).
- profitIfWon, LOST, PUSH (paid - stake = -fee), FMV (stake*(settleValue/cost - 1)), expectedProfit (stake*EV), the variance term and ClvPeriod day boundaries (DST-safe) are correct. VOID is excluded in ClvStats and BetTracker.stats (but see the VOID finding for the file).
- Captured closes are properly dated by the oldest book price (asOf), the supersedes() rule keeps the freshest, and the 15-minute window plus the final read near T-110 s is a sound design. The test 'a bet placed in the last minutes with nothing read since has no close' prevents the 'closed at its own line' artifact; I could not find a path where observe() writes the bet's own fair as its close in the same scan.
- Caveats, not bugs: CNO-page closes are dated by fetch time though CNO's data can be 1-2 min older (RESEARCH.md section 113); mergeReads averages CNO's and Vigilant's fair arithmetically for the close (a third definition); ESPN's close is DraftKings (a soft book) and the SGO/OddsPapi/Unabated/OddsPapi-v4 parsers have never met a live key (CLAUDE.md), so their CLV is unverified; CNO ✓ bets derive fairAtBet from CNO's integer-rounded fairOdds (up to about 0.25% relative error) while evPercentAtBet is CNO's displayed EV, so ev != fair/cost - 1 by a few hundredths of a point.

Recommendations for the export (not defects): put a summary header with (a) closed coverage = closed/(closed+missed), (b) CLV mean, median and stake-weighted, per closeVia and per closeTier (captured <=15 min / history), (c) CLV computed with one devig method, (d) counts of ties. Include closeSnapshotLeadSec for every source, closeDevig, evOutlier and clvOutlier flags, and countsInStats. Make clear that Novig-trade and ESPN-DK closes are lower-grade than Pinnacle/captured.
