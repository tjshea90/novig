## scout:age-signals

- **summary**: The app knows one CNO age per read, not more. A list read (CnoSnapshot) carries the phone's fetch time (fetchedAtMs) and CNO's own "Last Updated: N seconds ago" (cnoAgeSeconds); dataAtMs = fetchedAtMs - N s is the only "how old is CNO's data" value, and it is page-wide. There is no per-row and no per-book time anywhere: the game page's CnoBookPrice.atMs is never set, and CNO's fair carries only a book count. The list-level age is used consistently for the list gates: all CNO picks hidden past 5 min (cnoTooOld), a stuck banner past 10 min, and a 5 min (10 min when the game is more than 3 h away) fairAsOf check at order time (ApiBetPlanner). It is lost or weakened in six ways, all verified in code. (1) A missing or "Loading..." age silently becomes 0, i.e. fresh, so STUCK, too-old and the planner's "unknown age is not fresh" refusal cannot fire for CNO, and nothing counts how often that happens. (2) The game page is aged by fetchedAtMs (UiState.booksAt, ScanStudy, BetRecheck), not by its own Last Updated. (3) AtBet.cnoListAgeSec means two different things (placed bets: phone-side read age only; study first look: includes CNO's own age) and is recorded once at first look. (4) The CNO-listed Novig price is discarded when Novig's live book (read at most every 15 s on screen, once per background cycle for the top 10, accepted if no older than 60 s) is available; the two are never compared, only shown as "was +117", and nothing records how often they differ. (5) Diagnostics shows the phone's last read time but not CNO's Last Updated. (6) The only age split anywhere is the book page's. Measured so far: a past study of 505 CNO-sourced bets saw list age 0-107 s, median 6 s, never minutes, but that is the age at first look, which is biased low because a row first appears when CNO publishes it, and it says nothing about how stale CNO's underlying book prices are. CNO publishes every 13-33 s, irregularly, per a 2026-09-26 note, so a 15 s poll sees data 0 to about 33 s old. I did not probe CNO or search online (CNO's terms bar automated reads, and this task was read-only): whether a CNO price is already stale when read, by book and by kind, is NOT established. ## scout:bid-inputs

- **summary**: VERIFIED (read in code, nothing run, no file in /home/user/novig touched): the bid desk (MakerDesk, MakerQuote, MakerPlan, MakerGuard) never sees where a price comes from. It consumes `MakerLine`, which has 27 fields (16 without defaults), and the only Vigilant-specific code is `MakerLines.from(ScanResult)` plus three call sites in MakerRunner.kt (lines 141, 228, 282). A CNO-sourced line is buildable, but five things differ from a Vigilant line and each fails silently if copied naively.

1. Freshness is one number. `fairAsOfMs` is the OLDEST book quote behind the fair. `fairOld` is `now - fairAsOfMs > 5 min` (10 min if the game is over 3 h away). A bid's expiry is `min(now+ttl 30 min, start-15 min, fairAsOfMs+5 or 10 min)`, and a bid needs at least 60 s of life or the line is skipped. A CNO line's honest `fairAsOfMs` is `min(list.dataAtMs, dataAtMs of every game page that supplied book or sharp data)`, where `dataAtMs = fetchedAtMs - cnoAgeSeconds*1000` (CnoModels.kt:114 and 150). The 15 s list read does NOT make the fair 15 s fresh if the sharp anchor comes from a game page read every 1 to 4 min. The oldest input binds, and the existing lane re-reads pages only every 4 min (`AGREE_TTL_MS`, CnoFeed.kt:592).

2. CNO's age is knowable, but only as one coarse number. The list panel "Last Updated: N seconds ago" is parsed (CnoPage.kt:201) and is the only age. CNO publishes every 13 to 33 s (RESEARCH.md §19), so at a 15 s read the data is about 0 to 33 s old, typically 10 to 15 s (INFER). There is no per-book time on CNO: `CnoBookPrice.atMs` is never set by `CnoBooks.parse`. Whether CNO's own data lags the books is not knowable from the app. The game page's own "Last Updated" has never been verified live (RESEARCH.md §60.2).

3. The CNO list is a TAKER list. It only holds sides whose Novig take price is at least 0% EV against CNO's fair, and the app's code clamps the request to 0% or more (CnoClient.kt:280, and `"0%"` in the wide read at :327). The desk skips a bid priced at or above Novig's offer ("take it instead", MakerQuote.kt:546-547), so listed rows with EV at or above about the margin (4%) never get a bid. The natural bid targets, sides with negative take-EV, are absent. Rows with EV between minEv (1%) and about 4% are the only ones left. Both sides of a listed game are on its game page, which is the only way to widen this.

4. Fill judging is blind or biased. The picked-off guard needs `cycle()` to run (it does not when there is no Vigilant scan result: MakerRunner.kt:127-135 returns early), and the filled side must be in a later pass's lines with `fairNewestMs >= firstFillAtMs`. A picked-off fill's side is by definition negative-EV to take, so it is not in CNO's +EV list. The guard would only ever see fills that were not picked off, so it could never trip (INFER, high confidence from the code).

5. Wiring. With Vigilant's scan off the only trigger is the background cycle (AutoScan.kt:289-290), after the auto-bet, alerts and closing steps. `MakerSetup.set` flips the scanner back to BOTH (MakerSetup.kt:49-52), `cnoRead` reads no pages or live prices unless an alert or auto-bet threshold exists (AutoScan.kt:369), and `AtBets.bid` hard-codes scanner "Vigilant" (AtBet.kt:241).

All of Tj's bid limits live in `MakerRules`, `MakerPlan` and `MakerDesk` and apply to any line source unchanged (Q5). Rules that read missing data as "pass" must be switched on explicitly for CNO lines: `requireSharp`, a real `bookFairs` list, and a non-empty `sharpFairs`.
