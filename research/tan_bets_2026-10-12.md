## 2. MODERATE (act softly, mechanism known beforehand): player-prop OVERS win far less than their fair says
Settled, cluster-robust: Over props n=456 win 39.7% vs fair 49.2% (-9.5 pp), ROI -15.4%, z_game -3.4, z_day -2.5. Under props n=367: -3.0 pp, ROI -4.2%, z -1.3.
Same sign in all four sports (NFL -9.7, NHL -5.4, MLB -14.1, WNBA -17.6 pp); inside lead<13 h Over -10.3 pp (z -3.2) vs Under -0.2 pp (z -0.5).
Over bids (n=79) -6.9 pp, Under bids (n=86) -2.5 pp: the same direction for makers. Non-prop markets +3.1 pp (n=253).
Why believe it: books shade the public side (Over) so a devigged fair for an Over is a bit high; literature puts it at 1-3 pp, this sample says more.
Over-vs-Under difference is 1.4-2.2 SE (not conclusive alone), CLV does NOT see it (Over CLV +1.85% > Under +0.91%), because the close carries the same bias.
=> do not ban Overs; make the app ask Over props for a larger margin (see TAN3 actions).
