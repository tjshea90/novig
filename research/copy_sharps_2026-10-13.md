# Can the app find what sharps are betting and copy it? (Tj, 2026-10-13)
Verdict: identifying and copying individual sharps is NOT possible on Novig and the closest measurable versions did not make money; the part of it that does work (following the sharp books' prices) is what Vigilant already does. Evidence: RESEARCH.md §62 (59 days, 9.5M Novig trades, re-runnable with tools/research/novig_size_study.py), §116 (Novig lags Pinnacle ~6.6 s), NOVIG_API.md §10.

1. Plausible? Who: no. Novig's trade files and websocket carry outcome, price, size, time, but accounts are anonymized ("one bettor's record can't be followed", NOVIG_API.md §10) and the API cannot even list bets placed in the Novig app. Sportsbooks never expose bettors. Sharp signals that DO exist are indirect: size, price moves, and who posts liquidity.
2. Following big resting Novig liquidity ("syndicate liquidity"): measured -0.57 c / -1.05% EV per bet (n=5,922, interval excludes 0). It is quoting by market makers, not a side being backed, and a follower pays at least a tick more than the maker did.
3. Following big takers ($10k+): +0.55 c / +1.3% EV but the interval includes zero (n=562 over 59 days, ~9 a day), no NFL edge, positive only in a few MLB and college games; -7% at settlement in the small sample. Not a strategy.
4. Following sharp BOOK moves (Pinnacle/Circa steam): this is already the core. The fair line is the devigged sharp price; a Novig price below it is the bet. Study §116: Novig lags Pinnacle by a median 6.6 s; the edge survives a score-driven move, not a price-only move. Real-world check on live: 77 Pinnodds-live orders, 6 filled (5.8 s send-to-fill = Novig's own ~5 s delay), so the lag is largely unharvestable for takers in play. Pregame, steam is slower and the scan catches it.
5. What could still be worth testing cheaply (nothing built): (a) use PropLine's steam / line_movement events (pushed within seconds for DK/FD/Fanatics/Kalshi/Polymarket) as a TRIGGER to re-price just that market and as a "sharp money is moving this way" confidence input to the star rating; test on the scan-study data first (does a bet whose sharp-book line moved toward it in the last 10-30 min beat the close more often?); (b) Pinnacle's own limit size as a sharpness weight (Pinnodds / PropLine liquidity field); (c) reverse line movement is unavailable without public betting percentages (SharpAPI "betting splits" is a $229 tier, not worth it).
6. Profitable? Copying individuals: no data and no access. Copying sharp prices: yes, that is the existing edge; the scan study shows it as the +EV that beats the close. A further gain from steam timing is unproven.
