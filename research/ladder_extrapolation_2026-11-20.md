# Why 22% of the RFQ legs have no fair line, and how far the posted rungs can be extended (MK21, 2026-11-20)

**Trigger.** Tj 2026-11-20 (~00:00 UTC): "Both propline and parlayapi and also kalshi have sockets. That isn't enough to get fair odds for all these bets? Something is seriously wrong. The engine was able to price way more rfqs earlier."

**1. Nothing broke; two different things.**
- *Quotes fell from 11-19% of RFQs (paper, 20:30-22:15 UTC) to 0.03% (LIVE, from 22:16).* The paper "quote" ignored the wallet and tonight's options. Current job, weighted journal (the journal keeps every RFQ that has a fair and 1 in 25 of the rest): no fair line 38.2%, two legs of one game 22.0%, three or more props 12.5%, more than 7 legs 11.1%, **longshots-only 7.6%, cash short 3.8%**, slate 1.2%, too likely 1.0%, the rest under 1% each. What paper quoted (13-14%) is what longshots-only + cash + slate + too-likely decline now.
- *The fair-line gap is not new.* Weighted, "no fair line" was 38-47% of RFQs from 06:30 UTC on, and in the 22:39 job and the 23:25 job the leg coverage by league and kind is the same (NBA player props within 3 h: 27% and 23% priced; NFL props 77/78%, NHL props 75/74%, every game line 88-97%, NCAAB in-play 0-1%).

**2. Where the gap is.** Legs without a fair line (current job, catalog join): NBA player props (points 470, rebounds 158, threes 126, assists 94 per 2,454 sampled), NFL props (receiving yards, touchdowns, receptions), NCAAB in-play legs. The books post a player's stat at the main number and one or two rungs (Jalen Brunson points 25.5 and 26.5); Novig lists 14.5 to 34.5. Of 785 NBA prop legs of the journal grouped by game, player and stat: 44% are priced, **55% sit outside the posted range** (nearly half of those within 30% of the posted strike), 1% have no posted strike at all, and none sit inside it unpriced (the interpolating ladder prices those). So it is the books' coverage of ALTERNATE strikes, not the number of sockets: PropLine, ParlayAPI and Kalshi send everything they have.

**3. Can the posted rung price the rest?** Yes, with an error that depends on the side's probability. Method (`tools/ladder_extrap.py`): on Novig's own CLOSING ladders of 2026-11-15..18 (NBA about 2,100 player-stat ladders with 3+ strikes; NHL shots; NFL yards and receptions) predict each strike from the one nearest even: p(K) = cdf(probit(p_base) + beta x (L-K)/sqrt(L)), beta per stat fitted on two thirds of the games, error on the rest.
- Betas (gamma 0.5 beat 0.75 and 1.0 for every stat but NFL pass yards, flat): NBA points .515, rebounds .735, assists .775, threes .730, PRA .540, points+rebounds .490, points+assists .510, rebounds+assists .655; NHL shots .805; NFL receiving yards .180, rushing .200, passing .200, receptions .795, rush+rec .080.
- Held-out mean absolute error 2.4-3.7 probability points (NBA points 2.4, threes 2.9, PRA 3.5; NHL shots 2.8; NFL 1.9-4.3). No visible growth with distance up to the 3-unit range.
- Relative error of a SIDE (predicted over Novig's close): 0.25-0.50: sd 12%, p10 -7%; 0.50-0.75: sd 7%, p10 -11%; 0.75-0.90: sd 5%, p10 -8%; **0.10-0.25: sd 29%, p10 -14%, over-predicts by 11% on average; under 0.10: sd 69%**. Against the results themselves the model over-states long shots (predicted .177 vs .111 hit in 0.10-0.25) and under-states favourites (.82 vs .89).

**4. What was built.** `RfqLadder.extrapolate` + `Fairs(extrapolate = { cfg.ladderExtrapolate })` (CloudMain): a prop rung outside the posted range gets the model's fair, only when the side is 12%-92% by the model and within 3 units of the base; its label starts "extrapolated" and `LegFair.extrapolated` is set (the journal row's leg label shows it). It is a prop leg like any: the maker's prop premium (20% per leg, at most two prop legs) applies, which is above the 14% p10 error of the 10-25% band. Switch `ladderExtrapolate` (default on; `rfq_set.py ladderExtrapolate=false` = back to posted + interpolated only). Tests: `RfqLadderTest` (formula, bounds, off switch, mid-side only).

**5. What it will and will not do.** It raises the share of RFQs with every leg priced (the biggest hole), but the prop premium and the safe-edge floor still make a quote for an extrapolated parlay about 20-45% above fair on two prop legs: the market pays 0.3-1.3 points of pot over fair, so most of these quotes will not win the auction. Whether they fill, and whether they are right, is read from the journal: rows whose leg label starts "extrapolated", the market's EXECUTED price for them against our fair (`tools/rfq_summary.py`, OUTCOMES.md). **Revisit the 20% prop premium on this evidence, not before.** Note the realised results so far: 5 settled parlays, all five buyer hits (-$26.23): an unbiased fair would make that a 1-in-100 draw at the 30% hit rate; more coverage is not more profit until the fair's bias against buyers (favourites under-priced by the devig) is understood.
