# odds-api.net (the $50 Growth plan Tj screenshotted), vendor pages read 2026-10-13
Not The Odds API (the-odds-api.com) and not odds-api.io. All figures are vendor claims; nothing has met a key.
- Freshness: "odds snapshots commonly use 60 to 120 second polling"; "the fastest refresh target is once a minute"; leagues in priority bands (major leagues checked more, more often in the last hour before the start); "some sources stream changes, others are checked on a variable request schedule"; SSE/WebSocket streams on every plan with a resume token; IN-PLAY ODDS NOT SUPPORTED; 99.8% monthly availability target; no latency figures; the linked 7-day source-to-ingest study was not readable.
- Books: all bookmakers on every plan; counts inconsistent (125+ / 180+ / 100+); US 18, UK 17 books + 4 exchanges, Canada 16, Colombia 15, Australia 105. Confirmed by name: Pinnacle, Betfair (exchange), bet365, DraftKings, FanDuel. NOT confirmed: Circa, Bookmaker, BetOnline, Novig, ProphetX, Kalshi, Polymarket, Matchbook. The full catalog is only at GET /v1/bookmakers (needs a key).
- Pinnacle: pre-match lines only (spreads/totals/period/winner), no plan restriction; sports include football, tennis, baseball, basketball, American football, rugby league, Aussie rules, MMA; no mention of Pinnacle player props or alternate lines; "not Pinnacle's direct API".
- Sharp classification: the site never labels books sharp or soft.
- Pricing: no free tier; Starter $30 (50K req, 150 stream-hours, 1 stream), Growth $50 (250K req, 500 stream-hours, 3 streams, 120/min), Builder $90, Live $250, Pro $500. History is a paid add-on. +EV/arbitrage "Bets" feed counts against the request allowance.
Verdict: Pinnacle there is polled every 60-120 s (best case 60 s) vs PinnWire/pinnapi real time (free): worse than what Tj has. Not worth $50. Only value would be an extra independent Pinnacle/Betfair copy, unproven.
