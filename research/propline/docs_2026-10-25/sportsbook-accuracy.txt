How it works
Closing prices. For each book we use the last price it posted before the game started, on two-way Over/Under player props.
Same line, 3+ books. A line counts only when at least three books priced the same player, stat and number. That way every book is judged on the same bets, not on an easier mix.
Scored against the box score. We remove the vig, then score the Over probability against the official result (Brier score). Each book is compared with the average of the other books on that line. A book is marked “beats” or “trails” only when the 95% range is fully above or below zero.
Why most books trail. The average of many books is a better forecaster than almost any single book, so beating it at all is rare. Read the ranking, not just the sign.
Left out. DFS pick'em apps (their prices aren't real two-way odds). Exchange margins (Kalshi, Polymarket, Novig, ProphetX) don't include their separate trading fees. This is a pricing report, not a betting-profit claim.
